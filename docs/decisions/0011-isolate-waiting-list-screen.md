question from offer acceptance, functioning-transplant percentages and transplant receipt.

continuity. Combine only deceased- and living-donor removals at the reporting program for the

results. Select the latest three consecutive sufficiently covered comparison years without
looking at group outcomes. Report both comparison groups and an explicit continue, stop or
inconclusive verdict. Practical thresholds are screening choices, not clinical criteria.

boundary. It does not authorize a new model, source expansion, tracked release, app feature or
reinterpretation of previous results. Unresolved evidence or unfinished verification is a reason
for an inconclusive verdict within the session, not an automatic extension of the investigation.
