## Source/accounting coverage and reproduction

All 2,144 selected program-years (255 distinct composite program keys, 2017–2025) have complete
counts and exact accounting. There are 24 annual zero starting counts. Among 1,889 matched
consecutive pairs, 241 are excluded for mismatched year-boundary counts and 11 for zero current
starting counts, leaving 1,637 eligible comparisons. Retain these exclusions rather than treat
report revisions as activity. Of 1,195 later-vintage program-year comparisons, 290 disagree
(including absent programs); original values and changed fields remain in revisions.json.
The first year’s 242 “newly observed” programs mean first seen in this selected archive.
Later first appearances and apparent exits are listed by composite key in QA; they do not prove
opening or closure. There is no verified 2016 source and the two excluded releases do not
contribute analytical observations. See the [source audit](audits/waiting-list-source-0025.md).
